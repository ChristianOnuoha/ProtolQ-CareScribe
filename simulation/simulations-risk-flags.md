# Simulation Risk Flags — CareScribe

This document outlines the early risk detection signals used in the **CareScribe simulation**. The purpose of these flags is not to judge the quality of care provided, but to identify potential **documentation gaps** that could create compliance risks during inspections or audits.

> **Note:** This system does not replace professional judgment. It highlights areas where documentation may require further clarification before records are finalized.

---

## Core Documentation Risk Flags

### 1. Missing Outcome Documentation
**Signal:** An incident or intervention is recorded, but the follow-up action is not documented.
*   **Example Pattern:** *"Resident became agitated and required staff support."*
*   **Missing Clarification:** What action was taken? Did the resident settle? Was escalation required?
*   **Audit Risk:** Reviewers cannot determine if the situation was resolved or appropriately managed.

### 2. Escalation Gap
**Signal:** An incident involving potential safeguarding, injury, or behavioral risk is recorded without documented escalation.
*   **Example Pattern:** *"Resident fell in the corridor this afternoon."*
*   **Missing Clarification:** Was a senior informed? Was a health professional contacted? Was family notified?
*   **Audit Risk:** Inspectors may question whether safeguarding procedures were followed.

### 3. Context Deficiency
**Signal:** Observations are documented without sufficient context to understand their significance.
*   **Example Pattern:** *"Resident appeared distressed during lunch."*
*   **Missing Clarification:** What specific behavior indicated distress? Was it unusual? Does it link to a care plan?
*   **Audit Risk:** Documentation fails to show a clear understanding of the resident's specific needs.

### 4. Care Plan Link Missing
**Signal:** A behavioral or health-related event is documented but not linked to an existing care plan or risk assessment.
*   **Example Pattern:** *"Resident refused medication today."*
*   **Missing Clarification:** Does this align with the management plan? Does it represent a change in condition?
*   **Audit Risk:** Records appear disconnected from the resident’s documented care strategy.

### 5. Repetitive Narrative Patterns
**Signal:** Care reviews or notes contain repeated text across multiple periods with minimal variation.
*   **Example Pattern:** *"No changes observed. Resident stable."* (Repeated over multiple weeks).
*   **Missing Clarification:** Actual evaluation of the resident's current state.
*   **Audit Risk:** Documentation appears "templated" rather than reflective of active, person-centered care.

---

## Purpose of These Flags
The objective is to help care teams recognize where narrative gaps might unintentionally create compliance risk. By highlighting these areas early, teams can strengthen records while preserving professional judgment and workflow flexibility.

## ⚠️ Simulation Status
These risk flags are part of an **early-stage research simulation**. 
*   **Not for real-world deployment:** This tool is for studying documentation patterns.
*   **Research Focus:** Exploring whether structured review logic can identify gaps effectively in real care workflows.
