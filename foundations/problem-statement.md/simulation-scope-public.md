## 1. Purpose of This Simulation
The CareScribe simulation explores how **documentation gaps** in adult social care translate into **perceived compliance risk**. This is a controlled research environment designed to:

*   **Test assumptions** about documentation quality.
*   **Explore risk signals** within narrative records.
*   **Understand interpretation** of structured documentation by care professionals.

> **Note:** This is a research tool, not a production MVP.

---

## 2. Current Positioning
*   **Focus:** Problem clarity, pattern identification, and real-world validation.
*   **What it IS:** A research-led exploration of documentation visibility.
*   **What it is NOT:** A deployed AI documentation tool.

---

## 3. Who This Simulation Is For
Designed around **UK adult social care workflows** (CQC regulated), specifically for:
*   Adult social care workers & Domiciliary carers.
*   Care coordinators and Managers.
*   Compliance officers reviewing simulated documentation scenarios.

---

## 4. What Is Being Tested
1.  **Sample Input:** A sample care note is presented.
2.  **Logic Layer:** Structured “risk signals” are applied (e.g., missing follow-up, unclear outcomes).
3.  **Output:** Potential narrative gaps are highlighted.
4.  **User Feedback:** Does the note feel complete? Would it raise red flags in a CQC audit?

---

## 5. The Core Hypothesis
> **"Documentation risk is often a visibility problem, not necessarily a care delivery problem."**

This means care may have been delivered appropriately, but the **record** fails to demonstrate it. We are testing if simple logic patterns can close this "visibility gap."

---

## 6. Scope Boundaries (What it does NOT do)
To maintain ethical and research integrity, this simulation:
*   ❌ **Does NOT** generate notes from live conversations.
*   ❌ **Does NOT** provide real-time AI assistance.
*   ❌ **Does NOT** integrate with EHRs or live care systems.
*   ❌ **Does NOT** perform clinical or safeguarding decisions.
*   ❌ **Does NOT** store or process real patient data.

---

## 7. Compliance & Ethical Position
*   **No real patient data:** All scenarios are anonymized or synthetic.
*   **No Automation:** Decision-making remains 100% with the professional.
*   **CQC Alignment:** Conceptually aligned with regulatory expectations without claiming formal integration.

---

## 8. Success Criteria
This phase is successful if:
1.  **Recurring patterns** of narrative gaps are identified.
2.  **Care professionals** recognize these gaps as realistic and "high-risk."
3.  **Clear signals** emerge to dictate exactly what should (or should not) be built next.

---

## 9. Future Direction (Not Current State)
If validated, this research may eventually inform:
*   Structured note review systems.
*   Pre-submission quality checks for care records.
*   Documentation support tools for front-line staff.

**Operating Principle:** *Understanding the problem deeply is more valuable than prematurely building a solution in regulated environments.*
