# CareScribe — Documentation Support for UK Care Agencies

## The Problem
Care documentation is critical but stressful.
Staff are required to listen, care, and document accurately at the same time.
This leads to omissions, inconsistencies, and audit pressure.

## The Reality
- Notes are written live or immediately after care
- Accuracy depends heavily on individual staff
- Compliance issues often surface late during inspections

## The Risk
- Missing key information
- Inconsistent language across carers
- Stress during audits and reviews
- Operational time lost to rework

## The Direction
CareScribe is being explored as a documentation support layer:
- To reduce cognitive load
- To improve consistency
- To strengthen inspection readiness

## Pilot Focus
Early pilots aim to measure:
- Time spent on documentation
- Completeness of notes
- Perceived stress reduction

This is an early exploration focused on learning, not selling.
