# System Overview — CareScribe (MVP)

## Goal
To assist UK care workers in producing clear, compliant documentation
without removing professional judgment or introducing unsafe automation.

![CareScribe Workflow](ProtolQ-carescribe-MVPv1-workflow.jpg)

## High-Level Workflow

1. Care worker provides raw input
   - Voice note
   - Typed notes
   - Bullet points or rough observations

2. AI assists with:
   - Structuring content
   - Improving clarity
   - Aligning language with care documentation norms

3. Human review and approval
   - Care worker edits, confirms, or rejects output
   - No automatic submission or filing

4. Final documentation is exported
   - Used within existing care systems
   - Or copied into required platforms

## What the AI Does (MVP)
- Summarises raw notes
- Suggests structured documentation
- Improves clarity and completeness
- Flags missing context (non-diagnostic)

## What Stays Human-Controlled
- Final wording
- Professional judgment
- Compliance responsibility
- Submission and record keeping

## Explicitly Out of Scope (MVP)
- No medical diagnosis
- No autonomous decision-making
- No automatic filing into care systems
- No replacement of care staff workflows

CareScribe is designed as a **support layer**, not a system of record.
