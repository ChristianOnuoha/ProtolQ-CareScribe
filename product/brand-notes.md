# Brand & Messaging Strategy — CareScribe


| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Product Name** | CareScribe |
| **Parent Context** | Built under **ProtolQ**, an AI systems initiative by Ikesys Innovations Ltd. |
| **Audience** | UK Adult Social Care Professionals (Managers, Coordinators, Supervisors). |
| **Status** | 🟢 Brand Guidelines v1.0 |

---

## 🎯 Brand Positioning
**Measured, professional, and quietly authoritative.** 
CareScribe is not just "supportive"—it is trusted support in high-responsibility environments. It should feel:
*   **Trustworthy & Precise**
*   **Composed under pressure**
*   **Respectful of professional judgment**

> **Core Principle:** Make documentation risks visible before review. (Shift from *Relief* → *Visibility + Accountability*)

---

## 🛠️ Product Role
CareScribe acts as a **documentation review layer** that helps surface:
*   Missing context
*   Incomplete narratives
*   Potential inconsistencies
*   *Requirement: Highlights these gaps before records are reviewed or audited.*

---

## 🚧 Messaging Guardrails

### ✅ ALWAYS:
- [ ] Reinforce **human responsibility** and final professional judgment.
- [ ] Position CareScribe as **support**, not authority.
- [ ] Use soft-action verbs: *"highlights"*, *"surfaces"*, *"supports review"*.
- [ ] Emphasize: **clarity**, **consistency**, and **audit readiness**.

### ❌ NEVER:
- [ ] Claim "prevention" of audit failure.
- [ ] Suggest "guaranteed" compliance.
- [ ] Imply clinical or diagnostic capability.
- [ ] Position as a "replacement" for professional review.

---

## 🗣️ Tone of Voice
**Calm, but not soft. Clear, not simplistic. Professional, not corporate.**
> *It should sound like a senior colleague reviewing documentation—not a tech product selling features.*

### **Language System (Critical)**

| Use This 🟢 | Avoid This 🔴 |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Highlights potential gaps" | "Detects errors" |
| "Surfaces areas for review" | "Prevents issues" |
| "Supports documentation quality" | "Ensures compliance" |
| "Helps identify inconsistencies" | "Guarantees outcomes" |

---

## 🎨 Visual Direction
*   **Minimal, structured layouts:** Think "Professional Report," not "Social Media App."
*   **Clear hierarchy:** High readability for busy managers.
*   **Neutral Palette:** White, grey, and soft contrast (avoiding bright "Warning" reds).
*   **Process Diagrams:** Visuals should feel like clinical workflows, not tech architecture.
