What the MVP does

  • Record care visits
  • Generate structured care notes
  • Flag missing compliance elements
  • Highlight risks & next actions
  • Export as PDF or structured text

What it does not do

  • Clinical diagnosis or advice
  • Care Act or MCA assessments
  • Replace professional judgment
  • Store raw audio
  • Submit reports automatically
