# CareScribe — Agency Value Framing (Draft)

## Core Problem
Care agencies lose time, accuracy, and inspection confidence due to:
- Inconsistent documentation across staff
- Cognitive overload during live care
- Post-shift admin backlog
- Risk of omissions during audits

This is not a staff problem.
It is an operational system problem.

## Current Workflow (Observed)
- Notes written during or after care
- Multiple systems (EMIS, iStaffRota, paper → digital)
- High reliance on individual memory and wording
- Limited real-time consistency checks

## Organisational Impact
- Hidden admin hours
- Rework after audits
- Inspection anxiety
- Compliance gaps with third-party staff

## What Agencies Actually Care About
- Reduced admin hours
- Inspection readiness
- Consistency across carers
- Lower operational risk

## Future Pilot Hypothesis
If documentation support:
- Reduces cognitive load during care
- Improves structure and completeness
- Flags omissions early

Then agencies gain:
- Time back
- Better audit outcomes
- More predictable compliance
