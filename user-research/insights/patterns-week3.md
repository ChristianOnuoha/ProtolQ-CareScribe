## Strategic Discovery: Reframing the Problem
*Based on discovery sessions with **Care Coordinators** and **Clinicians**.*

### 1. Key Validations
* **The "Admin Time" Myth:** Feedback from Clinicians confirms that reducing admin time is secondary; the primary fear is that **Risk Exposure** persists even in "efficient" workflows.
* **Structural Failure:** Care Coordinators identified that compliance failure is systemic—individual "typing speed" cannot fix gaps in information ownership.
* **The Agency Gap:** Use of third-party staff creates a "visibility void" that leads to audit exposure.

### 2. The CareScribe Pivot
We are shifting the value proposition from **Documentation Assistance** to **Risk-Aware Documentation Intelligence.**

| From: Productivity Support | To: Risk Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Focus on "Admin time" | Focus on "Narrative accuracy" |
| Solving "Operational speed" | Solving "Information omission" |
| Assisting the **Clinician** | Protecting the **Agency System** |

---

## Current Status: Pilot Simulations
We are currently simulating these "Risk-Aware" logic flows to validate that catching **omissions** is more valuable to an agency than simply increasing **typing speed**.
