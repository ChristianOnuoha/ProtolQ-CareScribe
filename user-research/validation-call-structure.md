# 15-Minute CareScribe Validation Call

Goal: Validate admin pain + compliance risk (not features)

1. Context (3 mins)
- What’s your role?
- How often do you write care notes?

2. Current Process (5 mins)
- Walk me through how notes are written after a visit
- What usually goes wrong or feels frustrating?

3. Risk & Compliance (4 mins)
- What worries you most during audits or reviews?
- What mistakes are hardest to recover from?

4. Wrap (3 mins)
- Would you want help with documentation if done safely?
- Open to pilot or future conversation?
