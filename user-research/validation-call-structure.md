#15-Minute CareScribe Validation Call

Goal: Validate admin burden + compliance risk (not pitching)

1. Context (3 mins)
- What’s your role and setting?
- How often do you write care notes?

2. Current Process (5 mins)
- Walk me through what happens after a visit
- Where does it slow down or break?

3. Risk & Compliance (4 mins)
- What causes stress around audits or inspections?
- What mistakes are hardest to fix later?

4. Wrap (3 mins)
- If documentation could be safer + faster, would that matter?
- Open to staying in touch / pilot later?
