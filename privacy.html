Privacy Policy

ProtolQ CareScribe

Last updated: January 2026

ProtolQ CareScribe (“we”, “our”, “us”) is an early-stage research product. This policy explains how we handle your personal data during our waitlist and validation phase.

1. Information We Collect

When you join our waitlist or participate in research, we collect:

  • Name and Email address
  • Job role and organisation type
  • Feedback related to care documentation challenges

2. Data Minimisation & Simulation Safety

Important for Care Professionals:

CareScribe is currently a research and simulation tool. To maintain the highest standards of privacy:

  • No Patient Identifiable Data (PID): We explicitly request that users do not enter real names, addresses, or private patient IDs into the simulation.
  • Temporary Processing: Text entered into the simulation is analyzed for risk patterns but is not stored as a permanent medical record.
  • No Audio Storage: Raw audio used for research purposes is not stored on our production servers.

3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Under UK/EU GDPR, we process data based on Consent (when you submit forms) and Legitimate Interest (to improve the research tool). You may withdraw consent at any time.

4. Data Security

  • Data is stored securely using GDPR-compliant third-party tools (e.g., Tally.so).
  • Access is strictly limited to authorised research team members.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
