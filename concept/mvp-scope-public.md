# ProtolQ CareScribe — MVP Scope (v1)

**Document Type:** Public MVP Scope (Evidence)  
**Product:** ProtolQ CareScribe  
**Audience:** Advisors, Early Investors, Reviewers  
**Status:** Draft  
**Last Updated:** January 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose of the MVP

The purpose of the CareScribe MVP is to reduce documentation burden for UK adult social care workers by transforming visit conversations into **structured, compliance-aware care notes**, while keeping **human review and professional judgment fully in control**.

This MVP is intentionally narrow to maximise:

- Trust in regulated environments  
- Clarity of value  
- Speed of learning from real users  

---

## 2. Who the MVP Is For

### Primary users
- Adult social care support workers  
- Domiciliary carers  
- Community-based care professionals  

### Primary context
- Field visits  
- Routine check-ins  
- Follow-up and observation documentation  

This MVP is designed specifically for **UK adult social care workflows**.

---

## 3. Supported Care Note Types (MVP Only)

CareScribe v1 supports **only** the following three note types:

### Home Visit / Check-In Notes
Routine domiciliary visits and daily observations.

### Review / Follow-Up Notes
Post-incident or periodic reviews (non-clinical).

### Safeguarding Observation Notes (Non-Emergency)
Early indicators, concerns, or flags — **not** formal safeguarding referrals.

---

## 4. What the MVP Does

The MVP supports a single, simple workflow:

1. Record visit conversation (voice)  
2. Transcribe speech into text  
3. Generate a **structured draft care note**, including:
   - Needs  
   - Risks  
   - Observations  
   - Actions  
4. Highlight missing or incomplete sections  
5. Enable full human review and editing  
6. Export the final note (PDF or structured text)

**Core principle:**  
AI assists documentation — it does not make decisions.

---

## 5. What the MVP Explicitly Does NOT Do

The MVP does **not** support the following (by design):

- ❌ Full Care Act Assessments  
- ❌ Mental Capacity Act (MCA) formal assessments  
- ❌ Child safeguarding (adults only)  
- ❌ Clinical diagnosis or medical advice  
- ❌ Automatic referrals to councils or NHS systems  
- ❌ Real-time AI guidance during visits  
- ❌ Deep EHR integrations (export-only)  
- ❌ Team dashboards or analytics  
- ❌ Multi-language support (English only v1)  
- ❌ Automated decision-making or action enforcement  

---

## 6. Compliance & Trust Stance

- Human-in-the-loop at all times  
- AI output is **draft-only**, never final  
- No long-term audio storage  
- Users control when data is saved or exported  

Designed to align with:

- Care Act 2014  
- Strengths-based practice  
- Safeguarding awareness (non-decision support)  

**Disclaimer:**  
CareScribe does not replace statutory responsibilities or professional accountability.

---

## 7. Success Criteria for the MVP

The MVP is considered successful if:

- Users report time saved on documentation  
- Notes require less manual rewriting  
- Users trust the output enough to reuse it  
- The scope remains narrow, explainable, and auditable  

---

## 8. Non-Goals (Important)

This MVP is **not** designed to:

- Replace professional judgment  
- Automate compliance decisions  
- Serve as a full care management system  

These are future considerations — not MVP goals.

---

## Operating Principle

**Narrow scope builds trust faster than feature breadth in regulated systems.**
