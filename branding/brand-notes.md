# Brand Notes — CareScribe

## Product Name
CareScribe

## Parent Context
Built under ProtolQ, an AI systems initiative by Ikesys Innovations Ltd.

## Audience
- UK adult social care workers
- Care supervisors and managers
- Compliance-conscious organisations

## Brand Positioning
Calm, professional, and supportive.
Never futuristic, never hype-driven.

CareScribe should feel:
- Trustworthy
- Quietly intelligent
- Respectful of expertise

## Core Principle
Invisible technology. Visible relief.

## Messaging Guardrails
- Never suggest replacement of professionals
- Always reinforce human review and control
- Avoid diagnostic or clinical claims
- Emphasise time, clarity, and reduced fatigue

## Visual Direction (Early)
- Minimal interfaces
- Clear typography
- Neutral, calming colours
- Simple diagrams over complex visuals
