## Purpose
This document captures invalidated assumptions identified during early-stage research. These insights are critical to the evolution of CareScribe as they prevent premature product decisions and anchor our shift toward **documentation visibility** and **risk interpretation**.

---

## ❌ Assumption 1: Documentation is primarily a "Time" Problem
**Observed Reality:** 
Even when administrative time is relatively low (e.g., ~5% of shift time), documentation issues persist. 

**Underlying Mechanism:** 
The breakdown occurs due to **cognitive fatigue** at the end of a shift and **memory compression** across multiple visits. This leads to omitted details and simplified narratives.

> **Implication:** Reducing time spent does not resolve documentation risk. The problem is not time—it is **narrative completeness** under cognitive constraints.

---

## ❌ Assumption 2: Care Professionals Want AI to "Write" Their Notes
**Observed Reality:** 
There is strong resistance to fully automated documentation generation.

**Underlying Mechanism:** 
Documentation carries legal and professional accountability. Users must be able to stand behind every word written; trust breaks when the origin of a statement is unclear. Fully generated notes feel "unsafe" to the professional.

> **Implication:** The role of technology is not **authorship**—it is visibility support and structured assistance, not replacement.

---

## ❌ Assumption 3: Compliance Risk Comes from Major Errors
**Observed Reality:** 
In many cases, risk does not arise from obvious, singular mistakes.

**Underlying Mechanism:** 
Risk accumulates through small but critical gaps: missing outcomes, unclear follow-ups, and a lack of linkage between observations and actions. Under **CQC** scrutiny, these minor omissions become significant evidence of a lack of continuity.

> **Implication:** Compliance risk is cumulative and interpretive, not binary. The issue is not obvious failure—it is a **lack of visible continuity**.

---

## Synthesis & Strategic Direction
Across these invalidated assumptions, a unifying insight emerges: **Documentation risk is driven by a lack of visibility into narrative completeness and context.**

### Our Pivot:
*   **From:** Time-saving tool → **To:** Visibility system
*   **From:** AI Generation → **To:** Risk signal detection
*   **From:** Output creation → **To:** Documentation interpretation support

> **Closing Insight:** In regulated care environments, what is not clearly documented is often treated as not having happened.

### 📂 Source
